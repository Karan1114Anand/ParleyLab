       history: list[dict],

       outcome: str = 'deal_closed',     # or 'walked_away', 'timeout'

       user_brief: dict | None = None,   # {target, batna, ...}

   Returns dict with EXACT keys:

   - Outcome quality: did they close? did they get close to their target?

   fall back to move-quality-only scoring.

3. Best/worst move selection: use an LLM call with the full transcript
   plus the critic feedback per turn to identify the most impactful
   moves in either direction.

4. Rating thresholds:
   - 0-39   Poor
   - 40-59  Fair
   - 60-79  Good
   - 80-100 Excellent

   through scoring and print the result.
